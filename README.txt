Aurelia Gifts website
=====================

Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.

Before publishing:
1. Replace the placeholder Instagram handle.
2. Replace the placeholder WhatsApp number.
3. Add real product photos and prices in the Collections section.
4. Connect the enquiry form to your preferred email/WhatsApp form service.
5. Upload the folder to your web host.

The design uses an ivory, champagne-gold and blush palette inspired by the supplied Aurelia Gifts logo.
